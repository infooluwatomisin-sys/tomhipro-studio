About me

Design that helps your business feel as credible online as it is in real life.

I am Tomisin, the designer behind Tomhi Pro. I work as an Instagram carousel, logo and website designer, helping coaches, stylists, bakers and groomers turn their work into a brand people trust from the very first scroll.

Every project starts with your voice and your client, then moves into a logo, brand or site that is clean, warm and built to hold up on every screen it is opened on.

  • Custom designed, never a copy paste template
  • Built and tested across desktop, tablet and mobile
  • Designed to guide visitors toward booking or ordering

Questions

Still unsure about something? Send me a message and I will happily walk you through it.

How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start from $100 and full websites start from $250. The final price depends on how many sections and pages you need.

Do you design logos and brand kits too?

Yes. I design minimal logos, logomarks and complete brand identity kits with colours, type and mockups, on their own or alongside a website.

Who do you work with?

Coaches, consultants and service providers such as braiders, wig stylists, barbers, bakers and groomers. If you serve clients, I can help.

How long does it take?

Most landing pages are ready in a few days. Full websites usually take one to two weeks, depending on feedback.

Will it work on phones?

Every site is built mobile first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops before launch.

Where are you based?

I work remotely with clients worldwide, from New Zealand to Canada and everywhere in between.
